Empowering Youth & Job Seekers with Practical Industry Skills

✅ One-Line Summary: Don Bosco Skill Mission (DBSM) is a non-profit training institute that equips students and job seekers with practical, industry-ready skills and career support to help them secure employment.
📌 What is DBSM?

Don Bosco Skill Mission (DBSM) is a non-profit skill development and training center that provides industry-oriented training to help students and job seekers become career-ready.

🎯 Purpose
  • 🛠️ Develop practical job skills
  • 📈 Improve employability
  • 🧭 Provide career guidance
  • 🤝 Support placements and internships
📚 Courses Offered
☁️ Cloud Computing AWS, Cloud Infrastructure & DevOps
🌐 Networking CCNA, Routing, Switching & Security
⚡ EV Technology Electric Vehicle systems & diagnostics
🏨 Hospitality Front office & hotel operations
💻 Digital Skills Web dev & workplace IT tools
🗣️ Soft Skills Communication & interview preparation
💡 Why DBSM?
  • ⚡ Practical, hands-on learning
  • 📖 Industry-relevant curriculum
  • 👨‍🏫 Experienced trainers
  • 💼 Placement assistance
  • 💰 Affordable, career-focused training
👥 Who is it for?
  • 🎓 Students
  • 👨‍🎓 Fresh graduates
  • 🔍 Job seekers
  • 🔄 Career changers
  • 🌟 Anyone who wants to learn new skills
